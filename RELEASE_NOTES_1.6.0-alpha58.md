# Vector 1A 1.6.0-alpha58

## Director focus awareness

- Adds top and bottom Spatial Focus dwell/history to `/v1/state`.
- Reports current focus, time selected, immediately previous focus and its duration.
- Reports top focus strength/headroom and bottom focus strength/active Alpha window alongside history.
- Adds a reminder that Spatial Focus works best when electrode strength is properly calibrated in ReStim for the active configuration.
- ReStim remains the owner of electrode calibration; Vector does not duplicate or modify it.
